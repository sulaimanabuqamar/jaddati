JADDATI

Voices, carefully kept.

Keep the recordings you have of someone who has died, and hear new words in a recreation of their voice.

جدّتي — أصوات نحفظها بعناية.

What it does

You add someone, upload a recording you have the right to use, and Jaddati builds a voice from it. After that you can type anything and hear it read, or bring a children's book and have the pages read a page at a time — with a child able to stop and ask a question, answered in the same voice.

Recordings and clips stay in the app's own storage on the phone. There is no account and no server of ours. The three features that need a company outside the phone say so, by name, before anything is sent.
