Jaddati 1.0 · iPhone · iOS 17 and later

Delete a voice

Open the person, scroll to the bottom, and tap Remove this person. This deletes the cloned voice from the voice service first, then removes their recordings and clips from the phone. If the deletion at the service fails, nothing is removed here either — so try again rather than deleting the app, which would leave the voice there with no way to reach it.

If the app or the phone is already gone, write to the address above and the voice will be deleted.

Using the app

What recording should I use?

About a minute of clear speech from one person, with no music, no television, and nobody else talking. A voice message or an old video works well. Under twenty seconds the service will either refuse it or produce something thin, and either way a voice slot is spent finding out.

It says the recording may be too short

Below twenty seconds Jaddati will not upload it. Between twenty and forty-five seconds it will, but the result is usually worse than a longer sample. Try to find more audio of the same person before spending the slot.

Can I use it in Arabic?

Yes. Tap the globe on any screen, including the first one. Arabic is a complete interface, not English with the text pushed to the right — the layout, the numbers and the dates all change with it. Voices work in both languages, and the model chosen for speech handles Arabic including Gulf wording.

Nothing is happening when I tap a button

Check the home screen for Privacy and data. If it says everything is being kept on this phone, then creating voices, asking questions and speaking instead of typing are all switched off by your own choice, and the screens that offer them will say so. You can change it there.

What happens to the recording I upload?

It is sent to ElevenLabs to build the voice, and kept on your phone. The privacy policy sets out exactly what goes where.

The voice sounds wrong

Quality follows the sample almost entirely. Background noise, a phone speaker recording, or two people talking all show up in the result. A longer, cleaner sample of the same person is the only real fix; adding a second voice from a better recording does not delete the first, and each one occupies a slot.

Reporting a voice

If you believe someone has created a voice from a recording they had no right to use, write to sulaiman.abuqamar@gmail.com. Say whose voice it is and anything that identifies the recording. Voices are deleted on a credible report.

What Jaddati is not

It is a way to keep a voice and hear it read something. It is not therapy, not grief counselling, and not medical or psychological treatment, and it makes no claim to help with any of those. If you are struggling with a death, please talk to someone qualified.
