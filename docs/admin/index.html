What this page can and cannot see

The relay keeps one row per Google account that has signed in: the address, when it was first and last seen, how many clips it has made, and how many characters that came to. That is the whole record.

It holds nothing about what anyone said — no text, no audio, no titles, no names of the people in the app. Those never leave the phone or the browser they were typed on, and the relay never had them to keep.

A voice slot is released when the account gives it up, or when somebody new needs one and this account's is the one nobody has used for longest.
